Summer VR Project

VR experiences shaped by hand movement, visuals, and sound

2018–2019 · Collaborative work · Unreal Engine / HTC Vive / Leap Motion

Overview

A series of VR experiences built with Unreal Engine and HTC Vive. Table, Sensory, and Stage (2019) and CUBE (2018) explore hand movement, abstract spaces, and interactions between visuals and sound.

Table, Sensory, and Stage · 2019

A series of experiences for the 2019 Summer VR Project. The film brings together Table, using HTC Vive and Leap Motion, as well as Sensory, Stage, and additional mini projects.

Concept & design
Jeremy Mende, Rachel Berger, Tom Lim
Technology
Unreal Engine · HTC Vive · Leap Motion

CUBE · 2018

CUBE is a VR experience in which filling an abstract grid triggers harmonic and dissonant sounds. It explores how visual interaction and sound can create pleasing or discordant reactions.

Concept & design
Jeremy Mende, Tom Lim
Sound engineering

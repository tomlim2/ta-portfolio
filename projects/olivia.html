Olivia

A brand identity centered on natural pasture-raised living

Branding · Logo / Motion / Posters / Web design

Overview

Olivia is a brand identity centered on a joyful, natural, and healthy environment for chickens. The direction connects small pastures and consumers through a logo, color, typography, motion, posters, and a website design.

Context and direction

The project responds to distrust around chicken and egg production by focusing on the natural environment of small pastures. The research informed three core values: Natural, Impressive, and Trust.

About

I discover technology across different sources and test whether it serves the production process. I use working examples to discuss direction with colleagues, then turn selected approaches into tools and user-facing features. At Cinev Studio I developed NPR looks and character production pipelines; at Shotloom I designed and implemented retargeting and editing workflows for AI video production.

My background spans design, UX, engines, and web development. I consider visual quality, user workflows, and implementation constraints together, and document purpose, ownership, and validation so colleagues can continue the work.
